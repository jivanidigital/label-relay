## Reach and impact — final prepared wording

Jivani Digital Toolkit is a free, MIT-licensed project building practical automation tools for small D2C brands: https://github.com/jivanidigital/jivani-digital.

Our first tool, Label Relay, addresses a concrete operational problem: preparing shipping labels reliably without duplicate shipments, repeated downloads or manual distribution. Its components include retry-aware orchestration, lowest-total-rate selection, PDF composition, secure document links and email/WhatsApp delivery adapters, supported by 28 passing offline tests and GitHub Actions checks.

We are applying for discretionary consideration. The project is newly public and does not yet meet the published adoption thresholds. Our intended contribution is to make reusable, documented automation accessible to small teams that lack dedicated engineering resources.

Next milestones include completing the carrier integration and cloud deployment, validating the workflow end to end, and expanding into reusable WhatsApp automation, email automation and further D2C operations tools. We intend to maintain the toolkit publicly, welcome contributions and keep it free.
